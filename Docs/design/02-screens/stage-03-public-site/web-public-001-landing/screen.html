The problem

Building from abroad shouldn't cost you your savings

You can't see what happens on site. So you send money, ask for photos and hope. That's where it goes wrong.

Materials go missing

Cement, blocks and rods paid for your house end up on someone else’s site.

Quotes get inflated

Prices padded well above what materials actually cost in Enugu, with no way for you to check.

Photos get recycled

Old or borrowed pictures arrive as "this week’s progress", late and looking much like last week’s.

The damage isn't only financial. When trust breaks, family relationships often break with it.

How it works

Four steps. No money moves without proof.

See the full release flow
  1. 1

    Set up

    Invite your own contractor, agree a bill of quantities split into milestones, and pin your site on the map.

  2. 2

    Verified site visits

    A vetted, registered supervisor visits on schedule and unannounced, capturing evidence in our app.

  3. 3

    Milestone approval

    When your contractor says a stage is done, the supervisor verifies it. Then you review the proof and approve.

  4. 4

    Escrow release

    Only then does the licensed escrow partner pay your contractor. Not before.

Proof, not promises

Evidence that can't be faked

In-app camera only

There’s no upload button. Photos and videos can only be taken live, in the iBackHome app.

Stamped with place and time

Each capture records where and when it was taken, signed by the supervisor’s registered phone.

Inside your site, or rejected

Your site has a boundary on the map. Anything captured outside it is rejected automatically.

Reused photos get caught

Every image is compared with every photo on iBackHome. Look-alikes are flagged for our team to review.

Questions people ask

Is iBackHome a construction company?

No. You keep your own contractor. We verify what they claim on site and control when money moves.

Who holds my money?

A licensed escrow partner. iBackHome never holds client funds. Money is released milestone by milestone, only after verification and your approval.

Who are the supervisors?

Registered builders and engineers in Enugu. We check their professional registration, NIN and two guarantors, rotate them across sites, and send others on unannounced spot checks.

What if I don’t agree a milestone is done?

Raise a dispute instead of approving. The release pauses, and our team reviews the evidence with you and your contractor before anything is paid.

Can someone send old or borrowed photos?

Not through iBackHome. The app has no upload button, every capture must be taken inside your site, and look-alike images are flagged for review.

Where do you work?

Enugu, for now — including Independence Layout, GRA, New Haven and Trans-Ekulu.

What does it cost?

A monthly supervision fee per project plus a small fee on each release. See the pricing page for how it’s structured.

Start with your site address and your contractor's phone number. We'll handle the rest with you.
