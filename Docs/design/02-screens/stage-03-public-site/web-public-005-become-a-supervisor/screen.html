Become a supervisor

Be the eyes on site for families abroad

We're looking for registered builders and engineers across Nigeria to visit sites, capture evidence in our app and verify each stage of work. Paid per visit.

Apply to be a supervisor Check the requirements
Pay[₦ RATE] per visitplus transport · [placeholder until pilot rates are set]
WhereAcross Nigeria
ToolYour Android phone

Who we're looking for

  • Registered engineer (COREN) or builder (NIOB)
  • A valid NIN
  • Two guarantors we can contact
  • An Android phone (Android 8 or later) with a working camera and GPS
  • Able to reach building sites near where you live
  • Willing to declare any relatives who work as contractors

What you'll do

Scheduled visitsVisit your assigned sites and capture what you see.
Spot checksUnannounced visits to other supervisors’ sites.
Capture evidencePhotos and video in the app — it works offline.
Verify milestonesCheck each stage against its checklist, or send it back.
Confirm deliveriesCount materials delivered to site.
Review reportsCheck the weekly AI draft before the client sees it.

Independence is the job

Clients trust your verification because nobody can lean on it. These rules protect you as much as them.

No relatives’ projectsYou’ll never be assigned to, or spot-check, a project run by a contractor you’ve declared as family.
RotationSupervisors rotate after two consecutive milestones on the same project.
Checked by othersOther supervisors make unannounced spot checks on every site, including yours.
You never touch moneyYou verify work. Payments are approved by the client and made by the escrow partner.

How to apply

Apply in the iBackHome app. You'll see your vetting status there as each check is completed.

Start your application
  1. 1Sign up in the appWith your phone number and a one-time code.
  2. 2Send your detailsRegistration number, NIN, two guarantors and any relatives in construction.
  3. 3We checkOur team verifies each item. [TYPICAL TIME — TO BE CONFIRMED]
  4. 4Get your first siteOnce approved, ops assigns you a site near you.

Supervisor questions

Does the app work without signal?

Yes. You can capture with no signal; the app keeps each photo’s original time and place and uploads when you’re back online. Upload within 72 hours of capture.

Can I use photos from my gallery?

No. The app only accepts photos and video taken with its own camera, inside the site boundary. That’s what makes your evidence trustworthy.

How am I paid?

[PAYMENT METHOD AND SCHEDULE — TO BE CONFIRMED]

What if I’m turned down?

You’ll see the reason in the app and can apply again once it’s fixed.

Far from home, never out of sight. Client funds are held by a licensed escrow partner, never by iBackHome.
