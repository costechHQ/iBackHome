How it works

Every payment waits for proof

Your contractor builds. A vetted supervisor checks. You decide. A licensed escrow partner pays — and only when both the supervisor and you have said yes.

How a milestone gets paid

  1. STEP 1
    Contractor claims the stage Your contractor says a milestone is finished.
  3. STEP 2
    Supervisor captures evidence On site, in the iBackHome app only.
  5. STEP 3
    Three checks Every photo and video, automatically. Inside your site Device-signed Not reused
  7. STEP 4
    Supervisor verifies Checks the stage against the agreed checklist.
  9. STEP 5
    You approve From your phone, in three taps.
  11. STEP 6
    Escrow partner pays Your contractor receives naira. You get a receipt.
If a check fails Outside your site, wrong phone or wrong clock: rejected. A possible reused photo: flagged for our team. Either way it can't count — the supervisor captures again.
If you disagree Raise a dispute instead of approving. The release pauses. Our team reviews the evidence with you and your contractor, and two staff sign off the outcome: pay the milestone, or send it back for rework.

The supervisor can also send a claim back for rework at step 4. Contractor photos are shown to you for context but never count toward verification.

Before the first milestone

Setting up takes a few days, not weeks

  1. 1 Create your project Your site address, budget and the currency you’ll pay in.
  2. 2 Invite your contractor By phone or email. They join with a one-time code.
  3. 3 Agree the bill of quantities Your contractor prices the work in milestones. We check it against current Enugu prices.
  4. 4 Pin your site Mark the plot on the map. Evidence from outside it won’t count.
  5. 5 Fund the first milestone Pay by card in GBP, USD or EUR. The money goes straight to escrow.

While the work goes on

A weekly report, every Friday

AI drafts it from your project's evidence, verifications and payments; your supervisor checks it; you get it on WhatsApp, email and your dashboard. Every claim links to the photo behind it.

Unannounced spot checks

From time to time we send a different supervisor, without telling the contractor, to compare what's on site with what's been claimed.

Who does what

You Does: Fund milestones, review evidence, approve or dispute. Never: Has to take anyone’s word for it.
Your contractor Does: Prices the work, builds, claims milestones. Never: Approves their own work or sees spot checks coming.
Supervisor Does: Visits, captures evidence, verifies each stage. Never: Supervises a relative’s project or approves payment.
iBackHome team Does: Vets supervisors, reviews flags, resolves disputes. Never: Holds your money.
Escrow partner Does: Holds funds and pays out on release. Never: Releases without both approvals.
