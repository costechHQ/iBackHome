Trust and safety

How we protect your money, your site and your data

Trust is the product. Here's exactly who holds your money, how supervisors are vetted, why evidence can't be faked, and how your data is handled under Nigerian law.

Escrow Supervisors Evidence AI Your data Report a concern

Your money is never ours

Client funds are held by a licensed escrow partner from the moment you pay until a milestone is released. iBackHome never holds client money.

Escrow partner [LICENSED ESCROW PARTNER] Licensed by [REGULATOR] · Licence [NUMBER]. Named here once the partnership is signed.
  1. 1You pay by cardIn GBP, USD or EUR, through Stripe. Fees are shown before you pay.
  2. 2Escrow holds itConverted to naira at a recorded rate and held by the escrow partner.
  3. 3Two keys to releaseSupervisor verification and your approval. Either missing, nothing moves.
  4. 4Paid out directlyThe escrow partner pays your contractor or supplier in naira.
  • Every movement is recorded in a double-entry ledger you can view in your dashboard, with the exchange rate used.
  • Our records are checked against the payment providers and the escrow partner every night; differences go straight to our team.
  • A dispute pauses any release on that milestone until it's resolved, and resolving one needs sign-off from two of our staff.

Supervisors are vetted, rotated and checked

Supervisors are registered builders and engineers. Nobody is assigned to a site until our team has confirmed every check.

Professional registrationVerified with the relevant professional body.
Identity and guarantorsA valid NIN and two guarantors we contact.
No relatives’ projectsSupervisors declare relatives; they’re never assigned to or spot-check those contractors.
Rotation and spot checksRotated after two consecutive milestones, and checked by other supervisors unannounced.

Evidence that can’t be faked or reused

Every photo and video passes the same checks before anyone treats it as proof. A failed check is logged and the capture never counts.

In-app camera onlyNo gallery or file upload anywhere in the app.
Signed by a registered phoneEach capture is signed on the device with a key that never leaves it.
Inside the site, at the right timeRejected if taken outside the site boundary or if the phone’s clock has been changed.
Compared with every photoLook-alikes from any project are flagged for our team to review.

AI that drafts and checks — and nothing more

We use AI to write weekly reports, check quotes and flag suspicious photos. It’s labelled wherever it appears, and people make every decision.

Can’t move moneyNo AI can approve a milestone, release escrow or pay a supplier.
Numbers from the ledgerMoney figures in reports come from payment records, never from the AI.
Shows its sourcesReports link to the exact photos they describe.
Reviewed and loggedYour supervisor reviews each report before it’s sent; every AI run is recorded.

Your data, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. We ask for your consent when you sign up, collect only what the service needs, and log who accesses it.

Consent firstWe ask for your consent at sign-up and explain what each piece of data is for.
Only what’s neededWe collect the minimum to run your project. Personal photo details are removed from what clients see.
Private by defaultSite photos and videos are never public. Viewing links expire after 15 minutes.
Access is loggedSensitive actions are written to a log that can’t be edited.
Your rightsAsk to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by contacting our data protection officer.
