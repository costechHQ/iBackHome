Pricing questions

Which currency do I pay in?

GBP, USD or EUR, by card. Your contractor and suppliers are paid in naira, at the exchange rate recorded on the day you fund. You see both amounts before you pay.

Does iBackHome ever hold my money?

No. Your funds go to a licensed escrow partner and are released only after the supervisor verifies the work and you approve it.

Is the release fee taken from my contractor’s payment?

No. Fees are shown as separate lines when you fund a milestone. The milestone amount you agreed is what your contractor receives.
