Why good contractors like being supervised

Faster payment

When the supervisor verifies a stage, your client sees the proof straight away and can approve from their phone. The escrow partner pays you in naira.

Fewer arguments

"Is it done?" is settled by an independent supervisor and photos taken on site — not by long calls across time zones.

A record of good work

Every verified, paid milestone builds a track record you can show your next client.

Public profile coming later

Materials delivered

Request cement, blocks and rods from verified suppliers. Your client approves, the supplier delivers to site and is paid directly.

How it works for you

    Price the work Enter your bill of quantities, grouped into milestones.
  3. 3Phone
    Build and claim When a stage is finished, claim it from the app.
  4. 4On site

  5. 5Bank
    Get paid Once your client approves, the escrow partner pays you in naira.
What we ask of you
  • Let supervisors visit, including unannounced spot checks.
  • Take any photos you share in the iBackHome app — there's no upload from your gallery.
  • Expect your quote to be compared with current Enugu prices. Fair prices pass quietly.
  • If you disagree with a rejection, raise it with our team rather than with the client directly.

Contractors join by invitation

Your client creates the project and invites you by phone or email. You'll get a WhatsApp or email link and sign in with a one-time code — no password.
