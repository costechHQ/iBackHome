iBackHome
Design system v1.0·Stage 2 · Foundations and wordmark
Said "eyes back home"

Far from home, never out of sight.

Proof, not promises. A calm, card-based system built on one deep navy, cool grey-blue surfaces and very little else — so evidence and money are the loudest things on every screen.

Few colours
Navy for action and emphasis. Status colours live only inside small chips and alerts — never as large fills.
Proof is always visible
The three evidence checks — geofence, device signature, original image — look the same everywhere evidence appears.
Money says where it sits
Client currency first, NGN and FX rate beside it, and the escrow line wherever money is shown or moved.
00 · Wordmark

An eye inside a house

"Eyes back home", drawn with the same 1.6px outline as the icon set so it sits naturally beside the UI. One colour only: Navy 900 on light, white on navy. The word is Inter Bold at −0.035em, with no second colour or gradient.

Design system v1.0 · Stage 2

Controls

Buttons, inputs, choices, chips and badges, avatars and tabs. Shared by React web and mirrored in Flutter. Focus is always a 2px Accent ring with a 2px white gap; touch targets are at least 44px on mobile web and 48dp on Android.

01 · Buttons

One primary action per view

Sentence case, verb first. Raising a dispute or rejecting work uses Tertiary — it pauses, it doesn't destroy. Destructive is reserved for the confirm step of an irreversible action (revoke a device key, cancel an order, reject a supervisor) and always sits inside a dialog.
